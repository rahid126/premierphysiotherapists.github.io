Our philosophy

A modern approach to rehabilitation

We combine clinical assessment, therapeutic exercise, education and appropriate hands-on or adjunctive techniques to create a practical rehabilitation plan for each person.

Assessment-ledTreatment begins with understanding the person's symptoms, movement and functional goals.
Function-focusedRehabilitation is directed toward meaningful daily activities and participation.
